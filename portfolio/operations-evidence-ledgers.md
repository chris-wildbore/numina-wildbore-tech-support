# Operations Dashboards and Evidence-Gated Accomplishment Tracking

**Status:** Existing source-backed Google Sheets and Apps Script operations workflows, with historical acceptance; October 24, 2026 ledger readback. This is not a claim that every historical web-app deployment was live-tested on October 24.

## Challenge
Small organizations and individuals need practical systems that track operational changes and substantiate progress across activities without turning every email or draft into a claimed project launch.

## Approach
- Canonical spreadsheet records with source references, outcome status, human contribution and automation contribution.
- Lightweight Google Apps Script web desks as focused front ends rather than heavyweight custom infrastructure.
- Structured reconciliation across intake, tracking, status and follow-up workflows.
- Durable change logs and date-scoped closeout procedures so actions and decisions can be revisited without inflating counts.

## Evidence
A connected multi-sheet accomplishment tracker contained **1,522 dated entries** when audited October 24, 2026 (August 610, September 491, October 421). Records explicitly classify Chris-led, collaborative, and Vivian-led/Chris-directed work rather than attributing every action to automation. A prior public inquiry flow was accepted by the operator after a website form appeared in a private desk and a branded reply arrived through the intended channel.

The tracker is an evidence ledger, not a universal catalogue of commercially deployed software. Apps Script deployments are separately governed, and individual desks have different stages of readiness.

## Acceptance gaps and privacy
A previously recorded link audit covered only an earlier subset of entries; source links and intended omissions must be reconciled case by case. Live deployment status, end-user performance and independent client permission should be tested before stronger claims. No private spreadsheet rows, employment records, family notes, customer messages or third-party data are published here.

**Transferable result:** a lightweight, traceable operational reporting stack, with separate source, outcome, acceptance and attribution states.
