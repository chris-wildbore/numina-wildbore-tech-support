# Food Production Management Prototype

Status: **Application source and data model complete; deployment/acceptance pending**.

**Problem:** Repeatable small-batch food production needed defensible recipe lineage, scaling, allocation, and records.

**Designed:** Versioned recipes and ingredient details, run and run-item records, workflow steps, distribution, observations, audit events, validation and immutable completed runs.

**Evidence boundary:** The documented source uses sixteen Google Sheets tabs with a guarded Apps Script backend. That does not yet establish a deployed and accepted client application.

**Confidentiality:** Client/company names, recipes, operational documents, and any private distribution information are omitted until approved for publication.

**Pattern demonstrated:** Thoughtful data modeling, source authority, operational history, and validation-backed workflows.
